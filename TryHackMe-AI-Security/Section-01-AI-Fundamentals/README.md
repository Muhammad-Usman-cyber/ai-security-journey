# TryHackMe AI Security — Section 01: AI Fundamentals

## Overview

This section establishes the foundational knowledge required to understand
Artificial Intelligence and its security implications.

It is the first section of the TryHackMe AI Security pathway and covers how
AI systems work, common AI security threats, the importance of models and
data, prompt engineering, and the use of AI in digital forensics.

Rather than focusing only on theoretical concepts, I am using this section
to build the foundation required for deeper AI Security, AI Red Teaming,
and AI Defense work..

## Rooms Completed

### 1. The Building Blocks of AI

#### Basics

This room introduces the fundamental concepts behind Artificial
Intelligence, Machine Learning, neural networks, and Large Language
Models (LLMs).

Understanding these building blocks is important because security
professionals need to understand what they are protecting before they can
effectively assess the security of an AI system.

#### Usage

AI and ML systems are used in areas such as:

- Natural language processing
- Computer vision
- Recommendation systems
- Automation
- Threat detection
- Generative AI
- Intelligent applications

#### Future Impact

As AI becomes increasingly integrated into software and business
operations, security professionals will increasingly need to understand
how AI systems process information and make decisions.

#### My Learning

I learned the fundamental concepts behind modern AI systems and why
understanding AI architecture is important for approaching AI Security
from a cybersecurity perspective.

---

### 2. AI Security Threats

#### Basics

This room introduces the security threats that can affect AI systems and
explores how attackers can abuse weaknesses in AI-powered applications.

It provides the foundation for understanding AI as an attack surface
rather than treating it only as a technology that needs traditional
software security.

#### Usage

Understanding AI threats can help security professionals:

- Identify AI-specific attack surfaces
- Recognize potential attack techniques
- Assess risks in AI-powered applications
- Develop defensive controls
- Perform AI security assessments

#### Future Impact

As organizations increasingly deploy AI-powered applications and
services, AI-specific threats will become an important part of modern
security assessments and defensive operations.

#### My Learning

I learned that securing AI systems requires understanding both
traditional application security and threats that are specific to AI
systems.

This changed my perspective from simply using AI tools to thinking about
how those tools and systems can be attacked and defended.

---

### 3. AI Models & Data

#### Basics

This room focuses on the importance of models and data within AI systems.

AI systems depend heavily on the quality, integrity, and security of the
data used to build and operate them.

#### Usage

Models and data are fundamental to applications involving:

- Machine Learning
- Generative AI
- Natural Language Processing
- Computer Vision
- AI-powered security systems
- Recommendation and decision-making systems

#### Future Impact

As organizations become increasingly dependent on AI-driven decisions,
protecting models and their underlying data will become an important
security requirement.

Compromised, manipulated, or poorly protected data can affect the
behavior and reliability of AI systems.

#### My Learning

I learned that AI security is not limited to the model itself.

The surrounding data, training process, infrastructure, and application
environment are also important parts of the security landscape.

---

### 4. Prompt Engineering

#### Basics

Prompt engineering focuses on designing effective instructions for
Large Language Models and understanding how prompts influence model
behavior.

From a security perspective, understanding prompts is important because
attackers can also manipulate instructions to influence AI systems.

#### Usage

Prompt engineering is used for:

- Communicating effectively with LLMs
- Improving AI-generated results
- Building AI applications
- Testing AI behavior
- Security research and adversarial testing

#### Future Impact

As LLMs become integrated into applications, understanding how users,
applications, and external inputs influence model behavior will become
increasingly important for AI security testing.

#### My Learning

I learned that prompts are not simply questions given to an AI model.

They can influence model behavior significantly, making an understanding
of prompt construction valuable for both AI application development and
security testing.

This also provides a foundation for deeper study of prompt injection and
other LLM security issues.

---

### 5. AI Forensics

#### Basics

AI Forensics explores how AI can support digital forensic and
investigative activities.

It introduces the idea of using AI capabilities to assist security
professionals in analyzing information and identifying useful evidence.

#### Usage

AI can assist with activities such as:

- Large-scale data analysis
- Evidence organization
- Pattern identification
- Investigative assistance
- Digital forensic workflows
- Security investigation

#### Future Impact

As the amount and complexity of digital evidence continues to increase,
AI-assisted forensic analysis could help investigators process large
datasets more efficiently.

However, security professionals will still need to validate AI-generated
results and maintain proper investigative procedures.

#### My Learning

I learned how AI can complement traditional digital forensics and
investigative workflows.

An important takeaway is that AI should assist an investigation rather
than automatically replace human validation and professional judgment.
